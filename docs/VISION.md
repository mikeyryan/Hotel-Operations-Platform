# Product Vision

## Vision statement

For hotel staff and managers who need to coordinate operational work across a property, Hotel Operations Platform is a mobile-first request and task system that captures issues where they happen, makes ownership visible, and converts operational history into actionable insight.

Unlike informal chat groups, calls, or paper logs, it creates a searchable record of what happened, where it happened, who handled it, and how long it took.

## Desired outcome

A hotel team should be able to answer these questions without chasing people:

- What needs attention right now?
- Who owns each task?
- Which requests are overdue or high priority?
- What happened in a room or location previously?
- Which problems repeat most often?
- Where are response times or workloads becoming unhealthy?

## Product pillars

### Capture quickly

Creating a useful request should take less than a minute. Photo, location, category, and voice input should reduce typing.

### Coordinate clearly

Every request needs a visible status, priority, assignee, timestamps, and activity history.

### Learn continuously

Once the platform has reliable data, analytics and AI can identify patterns and summarize operations. AI suggestions must remain reviewable by a manager.

## Non-goals for the first version

- Replacing a full property-management system
- Guest booking, payment, or check-in workflows
- Predictive AI before enough trustworthy data exists
- Supporting every hotel department and workflow at launch
- Complex integrations before the core request lifecycle works

## Measures of success

Early testing should measure:

- Median time to submit a request
- Percentage of requests with a clear owner
- Time from submission to assignment
- Time from assignment to completion
- Percentage of staff who can complete the main flow without help
- Number of requests lost or duplicated during a pilot
